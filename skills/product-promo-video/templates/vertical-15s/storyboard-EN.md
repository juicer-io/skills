# Brand Listening video for Instagram Reels / TikTok · Storyboard (EN)

**For:** the marketing team; organic Instagram Reels and TikTok posts
**Status:** storyboard for review. Nothing rendered yet.

**Format:** 15 s · **1080×1920 vertical** MP4, 30 fps · motion graphics, no voiceover, no presenter · on-screen text carries the story · no setup steps
**Look:** Juicer navy + coral (references/brand-video-spec.md), white-text logo top-left
**UI:** one simplified Brand Listening card in the Juicer dashboard style (Website field → report → one mention). Real field and button names: **Website**, **Generate brand report**, **Latest mentions**.
**Data:** fictional hotel **Hotel Solenne** (`hotelsolenne.com`), labelled "Example data". Authors and handles are made up.
**Story:** one idea in 15 s: *type your website, see what people say and how they feel*. A longer 16:9 cut can cover the competitor angle. This short version leaves it out.

**Vertical safe zone:** IG and TikTok draw their UI over the top ~220 px, the bottom ~400 px and a ~140 px strip on the right (like, comment, share). All text and the UI card stay inside x 72–940, y 240–1500. The logo sits at y ≈ 250, not in the very corner.

---

## Scene 1 · Hook (0–2.5 s)
Big kinetic text, centered, three words per line, starts on frame 1 (no build-up, viewers swipe in the first second). Faint platform names (Reddit, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn) drift behind it.
**On screen:** People are talking / about your brand. / Are you **listening?**

## Scene 2 · Type your website (2.5–6.5 s)
The white Brand Listening card rises in at the bottom half of the frame. The **Website** field types itself:
> hotelsolenne.com

**Generate brand report** squashes. A burst of post cards with platform dots piles up around the card. Off-topic ones that only share the name ("Solenne album review", "Baby names like Solenne") grey out and fall away; the real mentions fly into the card.
**Caption (top):** Type your **website.**

## Scene 3 · The report (6.5–10.5 s)
The card turns into the report header:
- **264 mentions** counts up · 6 platform icons
- Sentiment bar: **62 % positive · 27 % neutral · 11 % negative**

**Caption:** Every mention, **in one report.**

## Scene 4 · Feelings, with a reason (10.5–13 s)
One mention card slides up under **Latest mentions** and gets the coral ring:
- **Reddit** · u/weekend_traveler · 2h · `Negative`
  "Waited 40 minutes at check-in and the room still wasn't ready."
  ✨ *Complains about slow check-in*

**Caption:** Know **how people feel**, and why.

## Scene 5 · End card (13–15 s)
UI leaves. Centered:
**On screen:** Hear it first. **Act on it.**
**Brand Listening** on Juicer · juicer.io

## Open for review
1. **Beta label.** This storyboard shows no label. Check whether the feature is still labelled beta or early access before rendering.
2. **Pace.** 15 s leaves ~1 s per caption beat. Scene 4 is the first thing to cut (to ~16 s total if it stays as is), or I can drop the off-topic flurry to give it more room.
3. **Time compression.** A real report takes a few minutes; here it takes ~4 s with no "sped up" note.
4. **End card URL.** `juicer.io` or the feature page `juicer.io/social-listening`? Links aren't clickable in Reels/TikTok, so the short one reads better.
5. **Sound.** Most Reels/TikTok views start muted-then-sound, so v2 gets a punchy synthesized bed plus SFX. Captions burned in already carry the whole story; separate `.srt` is still delivered for the TikTok/IG caption upload.
